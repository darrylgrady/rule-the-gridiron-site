Rule the Gridiron · football coaching game

Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

In short: Rule the Gridiron doesn't collect, send or share any information about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Your leagues are saved on your own device and nowhere else. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store.

What the app keeps, and where

Rule the Gridiron saves the leagues you play (teams, rosters, schedules, results and the names you give your team or coach) so you can pick up where you left off. These saves stay on your device:

  • Android app: in the app's private storage on your phone. If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include them in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; the game's developer can't see it.
  • iPhone app: in the app's private storage, which iOS may include in your iCloud or computer backups under your Apple account settings.
  • Browser version: in your browser's local storage for the game's page.

Deleting a league in the game deletes its save. Uninstalling the app deletes all of them.

Backups and names files

When you back up a league, the game makes a file and hands it to your phone's share sheet (or, in a browser, downloads it). You choose where it goes, such as your Files app, Google Drive or an email to yourself; the game doesn't send it anywhere on its own. Restoring a backup or loading a names file reads only the file you pick, and keeps what's in it on your device.

Purchases

Unlocking a dynasty or the full version is a purchase through Google Play or the App Store, under your Google or Apple account. The store handles the payment; the game only learns whether you own each unlock, so it can turn it on and restore it later. The developer never sees your payment details, name or email address. The store's own privacy policy covers what it records about the purchase.

Ratings

After a few wins the game may show the store's own rating prompt. Anything you write there goes to Google Play or the App Store under your account, as with any review; the game never sees it.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask for or collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It doesn't use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking services, and it doesn't share or sell anything.
  • The game itself doesn't go online: its fonts and data are all inside the app. The only connections are the store's, made by Google Play or the App Store when you buy or restore an unlock. (The browser version loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives the usual request information a browser sends, such as your IP address.)
  • It asks for no permissions beyond what every app has.

Children

Rule the Gridiron collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Rule the Gridiron changes any of this, for example by adding an online feature, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
